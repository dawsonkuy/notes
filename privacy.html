Privacy Policy

A clear explanation of how Bestpad handles your account, notes, device data, and privacy choices.

Effective November 11, 2026 · Last updated November 11, 2026

1. About Bestpad

Bestpad is a note-taking application available through the website at bestpad.app and its mobile app. This policy describes what information Bestpad processes, why it is used, where it is stored, and how you can delete it.

You can use Bestpad without creating an account. Creating an account enables cloud syncing between supported devices.

2. Information we process

Account information

If you register or sign in, Bestpad uses your email address, an account identifier, and authentication/session information to manage your account, verify sign-ins, and sync your notes. Account authentication is provided by Supabase. Passwords are handled by the authentication service; Bestpad does not put plaintext passwords in your notes database.

Notes and cloud sync

When you are signed in, the text of your notes, note titles, formatting, checklist and Trash status, note order, active note, and sync metadata such as revisions and update times can be stored in Bestpad's cloud database. Notes are associated with your account and are not intentionally published to other Bestpad users.

Guest notes are normally stored on the device in browser storage or app storage. On the website, if you sign into an account with no existing cloud workspace, guest notes from that browser may be imported into the account. The mobile app maintains separate guest and signed-in workspaces.

Device preferences and technical data

Theme choices, selected colors, and font preferences are stored on the device. The app and its infrastructure providers may process technical data such as IP addresses, browser or device information, request timestamps, and error/security logs to deliver, protect, and troubleshoot the service. We do not use note contents for targeted advertising.

3. How information is used

  • To register and authenticate accounts.
  • To save notes, maintain local recovery copies, and synchronize account notes across devices.
  • To support features you choose, including exporting or sharing a note using your device's share options.
  • To maintain service availability, detect abuse, investigate errors, and meet applicable legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal information or note contents, and Bestpad does not use your notes to train an AI model.

4. Service providers and sharing

We use Supabase for authentication and cloud note storage, and Cloudflare to deliver the Bestpad website. The website also loads some JavaScript libraries using jsDelivr. These infrastructure providers may receive the information needed to operate their services, including ordinary connection and security logs. We expect service providers to protect information appropriately for their roles.

If you choose to share a note using your device's share sheet, the selected destination service may receive the note content you choose to share; its own terms and privacy policy apply. We may disclose information where required by law or necessary to protect the service and its users.

5. Security and storage

Bestpad uses encrypted HTTPS connections for cloud requests and database access controls that limit accounts to their own synced workspaces. Your synced notes are not end-to-end encrypted, so do not use Bestpad as a password vault or a place to store highly sensitive secrets. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

Local notes and recovery copies remain on the device or browser where they were saved. Downloaded or shared copies are outside Bestpad's cloud deletion process.

6. Retention and account deletion

Synced notes are retained while your account exists, unless you delete them. Notes moved to Trash remain in the workspace until permanently removed or the account is deleted.

In the mobile app, go to Settings → Account deletion → Delete account. On the website, open Account → Delete account. Once confirmed and successfully processed, Bestpad deletes the authentication account and its associated synced notes, including Trash. Local guest notes and appearance preferences are kept; certain device-local recovery copies may require you to clear app or browser storage yourself. See the account deletion guide.

Infrastructure providers may retain limited backup or security log data temporarily under their retention policies or as legally required. Downloaded exports, shares to other apps, and device backups must be managed separately.

7. Your choices

You can use Bestpad without an account, sign out, export or share individual notes, remove notes, or delete your account. You can clear local application data using your device or browser settings, but this can permanently delete any unsynced notes, so back up important notes first.

For other privacy questions or requests, use the Bestpad support channel. It is a public GitHub issue tracker: do not post passwords, note contents, or other sensitive personal information there.

8. Children and changes

Bestpad is not directed to children under age 13, and we do not knowingly request personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has created an account, contact support.

We may update this policy as the product changes. Material changes will be reflected here with a new effective date, and additional notice will be provided where legally required.
