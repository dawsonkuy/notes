Account Deletion

How to permanently delete your Bestpad account and its cloud notes, with no support ticket required.

Effective November 11, 2026 · Last updated November 11, 2026

Delete your Bestpad account

Deleting your account permanently removes your Bestpad sign-in and cloud-synced notes, including notes in Trash. This action cannot be undone.

In the mobile app

  1. Open Bestpad and sign in to the account you want to delete.
  2. Tap Settings.
  3. Under Account deletion, tap Delete account.
  4. Review the warning and confirm Delete account. Wait for the confirmation that deletion has completed.

On the website

  1. Go to bestpad.app and sign in.
  2. Open Account in the top navigation.
  3. Select Delete account and confirm.
Open Bestpad

What is deleted and what stays

  • Deleted: Your Bestpad authentication account and synced workspace, including note text, names, formatting, and Trash.
  • Normally kept on your device: Guest notes, visual preferences, and files you exported or shared outside Bestpad.
  • May remain temporarily: Limited infrastructure backups or security logs retained by service providers according to their retention policies or legal requirements.

The app attempts to clear its account-specific recovery copy after confirmed deletion. If device storage cleanup fails, a local copy may remain on that device. Uninstalling Bestpad or clearing browser data by itself does not delete a cloud account.

Before you delete

If you want to keep your notes, export or share them before confirming deletion. Check that any unsynced changes are saved or backed up, especially if you see an offline or cloud-conflict message.

If you use Bestpad on multiple devices, any signed-in device may still hold a local cached copy. Account deletion removes the synced workspace; manage those device-specific copies separately.

Need help?

If you cannot access your account, use Bestpad support to report the problem. Support requests in GitHub are public, so do not include note contents, passwords, or other sensitive personal information. You do not need to contact support to delete an account when you can sign in.
