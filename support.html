Help & support

Helpful answers for saving, syncing, and managing your Bestpad account.

Notes aren't syncing

Confirm you're signed in to the same account on every device. Look for the Saved to cloud status before switching devices. If you see a cloud conflict, export or download both copies before choosing which version to keep. Never clear app or browser storage while unsynced changes remain.

Forgot your password?

Open Bestpad's login screen, enter your email address, and choose Forgot password?. Follow the email link to bestpad.app to set a new password. Then return to the app and sign in again. Check your spam folder if the email does not arrive.

Exporting your notes

On iPhone, open Notes → Export all to create and share a JSON copy of the entire current workspace, including notes in Trash. Keep a copy somewhere safe before reinstalling or replacing cloud-conflicted notes. Exports contain your private notes in readable form; share them only with services you trust.

Delete your account

Open Settings → Account deletion in the mobile app. Account deletion permanently removes your login and its cloud notes. You can also delete your account from the website's Account menu. Read the deletion guide first.

Report a problem

For general bugs and feature issues, open a ticket in the Bestpad public support tracker. Explain which device you use and what happened, without revealing any sensitive data.

Privacy note: GitHub issues are public. Do not include your email address, passwords, note contents, session tokens, or account identifiers in a support issue. Sensitive support requests need a private channel; do not submit them through the public tracker.
